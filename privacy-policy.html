Privacy Policy

HomePrivacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SnipThinK Software Private Limited ("we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects information when you use the Social MapWork mobile app (listed on Google Play as "Social MapWorK", package com.snipthink.socialmapwork) and this website. Social MapWork lets people report local civic problems on a shared map, report disasters, ask for and offer help (SOS), and optionally receive accident alerts.

In short: we use your location only to help you and your community, never to track you. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not collect your advertising ID.

1. Who we are and how to contact us

The app is developed and operated by SnipThinK Software Private Limited, India. For any privacy question, request or complaint, including requests to access, correct or delete your data, email support@socialmapwork.com. This address also acts as our grievance contact. We reply within 30 days.

3. How we use your location

  • While you use the app (foreground): to add your position to reports you submit, show problems near you on the map, and, if you turn on "nearby report questions", ask you to confirm reports close to you. For those questions the app downloads open reports in a small area (about 100 m) around you; the distance check happens on your phone.
  • In the background (Help people nearby is on by default for signed-in users after you allow location; the others only if you turn them on): Social MapWork collects location data to enable Help people nearby (SOS alerts), nearby report questions and Accident alerts, even when the app is closed or not in use. Before asking Android for "Allow all the time", the app shows a screen explaining this, and you can say no. Background location is not used for anything else.
  • What leaves your phone: the precise location of reports, disaster reports and SOS requests you send. For Help people nearby, only an approximate area (about 5 km cells) is used to deliver alerts. For Accident alerts, your location is sent to your emergency contacts by SMS only after a detected crash that you did not cancel.
  • We never track you all the time, build a location history of you, sell location data, or use it for advertising.

You can stop location use at any time by turning these features off in the app (Profile) or by changing the location permission in Android settings.

5. What is public

Social MapWork is a community map. The following can be seen by other users and the public:

  • Reports and disaster reports: category, details, location, time and status. Your display name is shown unless you report anonymously.
  • Your public profile: display name, username, profile photo, and your activity and coins.
  • Evidence photos only after you choose to share them.

Verified community leaders and authorities who handle reports can see additional report details (reporter identity, precise location and accuracy, capture time and device information) to verify reports, prevent abuse and resolve problems. SOS details are shown only to helpers near you while the SOS is active.

6. Sharing and third-party services

We do not sell your personal data and do not share it for advertising. We share data only:

  • With other users, as described in "What is public".
  • With your emergency contacts, only when Accident alerts detect a crash you did not cancel.
  • With service providers that run the app for us under their own privacy and security terms:
    Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Realtime Database, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics, Analytics, Performance Monitoring, App Check), Google Maps Platform, Google Sign-In, and Google Play services (location and Play Integrity). Phone sign-in codes are delivered by SMS through mobile networks. See Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information.
  • When required by law, or to protect the safety, rights or property of users, the public or us (for example, in response to a valid legal request from a court or law-enforcement authority in India).

7. Where data is stored and how it is protected

Data is kept on your phone and on Google Firebase servers. Our database and file storage are located in India (Mumbai region). Some Google services, such as sign-in, notifications and crash reporting, may process data in other countries under Google's safeguards.

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and is encrypted at rest by Google. Access is restricted by security rules: private settings, including your emergency contacts backup, can be read only by you. Emergency contacts are also stored encrypted on your phone. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you and the authorities of a data breach as required by law.

8. How long we keep data

  • Account and profile: until you delete your account.
  • Reports: kept on the map as a public safety record. When you delete your account, your name, user ID and contact phone are removed from them.
  • SOS location: erased when the SOS ends, or after 30 minutes.
  • Accident data: sensor data is never uploaded. Crash events are not stored on our servers.
  • Diagnostics: kept for the periods set by Google Firebase, then deleted automatically.
  • After account deletion: some records may be kept for up to 90 days where needed to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud and abuse, or keep the service secure. They are then permanently deleted.

9. Deleting your account and data

In the app: open Profile and tap Delete account. This deletes your profile, username, private settings (including the emergency contacts backup), uploaded photos and sign-in account, and removes your identity from your reports.

Without the app: email support@socialmapwork.com with the subject "Delete my account". Send it from the email address of your Google sign-in, or include the phone number you signed in with. We may ask you to confirm the request, and we complete it within 30 days.

You can also ask us to delete specific data, such as a report or a shared photo, without deleting your account. Data kept after deletion is described in "How long we keep data". Step-by-step instructions: Delete your account.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Pause or turn off Help people nearby, and turn off Accident alerts and nearby report questions, at any time in the app.
  • Allow or deny any permission in Android settings. Features that need a denied permission will not work, but the rest of the app will.
  • Report anonymously, and choose whether to share photos or your phone number.
  • Under applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, nominate someone to act for you, and raise a grievance. Email support@socialmapwork.com. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

11. Children

Social MapWork is for adults 18 years and older. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us personal data, email support@socialmapwork.com and we will delete it. See our Child Safety Standards.

12. Emergency features: important limits

Community helpers are volunteers, not emergency services. Accident alerts are an automatic estimate from your phone's sensors: they can miss real accidents and can trigger by mistake, and they do not contact emergency services. In danger, always call 112.

13. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change and change the effective date above. If a change affects how we use your data, the app will show it to you and ask you to accept it again before you continue.
