Report problems

A problem nobody reports is a problem nobody fixes. Your report puts it on the map, at its exact spot.

  1. Open the app and tap to add a report.
  2. Pick the category and add details.
  3. Take an evidence photo if you can, then submit.

Clean your area

Clean streets start with one spot on the map. Show where a bin or a clean-up is needed, and get your neighbours to join.

  1. Report a garbage pile, dump or "dustbin required" spot.
  2. Ask friends nearby to confirm it.
  3. Follow it on the map until it turns green.
Start this mission

Accident alerts

In an accident, minutes matter. If the app detects a crash and you do not cancel, it texts your location to your emergency contacts and calls the first one.

  1. Open Profile and turn on Accident alerts.
  2. Add emergency contacts who agree to be alerted.
  3. Allow the permissions the app asks for.

Social MapWork is not an emergency service. In danger, always call 112.

Start this mission

Be a helper

Help is often just a street away. With Help people nearby on, you can see SOS requests near you and choose to respond.

  1. Keep Help people nearby on in Profile.
  2. Allow location so requests near you can reach you.
  3. When an SOS comes in, accept it only if you can help safely.

Social MapWork is not an emergency service. In danger, always call 112.

Start this mission

Disaster help

In floods, fires or storms, rescuers need to know where help is needed. Report what is needed: water, food, shelter, medical or rescue.

  1. Open the app and report the disaster.
  2. Choose the type of help needed.
  3. Stay safe and follow official instructions.

Social MapWork is not an emergency service. In danger, always call 112.

Start this mission

Hold it accountable

Reports are verified by the community and handled by verified leaders and authorities, so every problem is followed until it is solved.

  1. Confirm or reject reports close to you.
  2. Follow the status of the problems you reported.
  3. Thank the people and authorities who fixed them.

How a problem gets solved

Every report is followed, step by step, until it is fixed.

  1. 01

    Report

    You pin the problem at its exact spot, with details and a photo.

  2. 02

    Verify

    People nearby confirm it is real, so fake reports are filtered out.

  3. 03

    Act

    Verified community leaders and authorities take it up.

  4. 04

    Fixed

    The spot turns green on the map for everyone to see.

Strength comes from numbers. The Roman army knew that and that's what we believe in too. We invented Social MapWork to connect citizens based on common/locality based issues. By Reporting Common Problem of Society(Locality) we can initiate movement, take action and therefore solve issues for the betterment of society, making us responsible citizens in its wake. Before Social MapWorK the process of reporting local social issues to the authorities or concerned organization was a tough and lengthy process (writing a letter or trying to find email addresses to address). This App easily Reports Common Social Issue in Just 4 Click, with better and specific data and information.
