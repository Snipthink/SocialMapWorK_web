    Help is on the way

    Nobody cancels. Without anyone touching it, your phone texts your location to your emergency contacts and calls the first one, from your own phone. They know where you are, and can come or call 112.

Private by design. Motion data is processed on your phone and never uploaded. Your location is sent only to your contacts, and only after a crash you did not cancel.

Good to know. Accident alerts are an automatic estimate: they can miss a real accident or trigger by mistake, and they depend on your battery settings, network and GPS. They do not contact emergency services. Without SMS permission, a ready-to-send message opens instead. Normal SMS and call charges apply. In danger, always call 112.

SOS and Help people nearby

Need help? People nearby can come.

Help is often just a street away. SOS connects you with neighbours who are ready to help.

  1. You press SOS

    Choose the help you need. Your location is shared only for this SOS.

  2. Neighbours get an alert

    People nearby who have Help people nearby on are alerted, even if their app is closed.

  3. A helper accepts

    Someone who can help safely accepts your SOS and comes to you.

  4. You end it safely

    Set a safety PIN: if someone forces you to cancel without it, your SOS stays active for helpers.

Your location is not kept. It is erased when the SOS ends, or automatically after 30 minutes. SOS details are shown only to helpers near you while the SOS is active.

Helpers are volunteers, not emergency services, and nobody can promise someone will respond. False alarms can be reported, and repeated false alarms can pause SOS on an account. In danger, always call 112.

Questions people ask

Does accident alert work when the app is closed?

Yes. Once you turn it on, it keeps running in the background with a notification always showing, and it stays on after your phone restarts.

What if it goes off by mistake?

A countdown appears first. Tap I'm OK to cancel it, and nothing is sent to anyone.

Will it send the message if I can't touch my phone?

Yes, if you allowed the SMS permission during setup. Without it, a ready-to-send message opens for you to send yourself.

Does it call an ambulance or the police?

No. It texts your location to your emergency contacts and calls the first one. They, or you, should call 112.

Does it cost anything?

The SMS and call are made from your own phone, so your mobile operator's normal charges apply.

Who sees my reports?

Reports are public on the map so problems can be fixed. Your name is shown unless you report anonymously.

Who can use Social MapWork?

Adults 18 and older. Anyone can view the map; reporting, SOS and accident alerts need an account verified with a phone number or Google.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Profile and tap Delete account. See Delete your account for other options.
