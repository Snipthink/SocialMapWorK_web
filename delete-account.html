Delete your Social MapWork account

HomeDelete account

This page explains how to delete your account and data for the Social MapWork app (listed on Google Play as "Social MapWorK", package com.snipthink.socialmapwork), developed by SnipThinK Software Private Limited.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open the Social MapWork app and go to Profile.
  2. Tap "Delete account" and confirm.
  3. If asked, sign in again to confirm it's you.

Your account is deleted straight away. This is the fastest way.

Don't have the app anymore?

If you uninstalled the app or lost your phone, email us and we will delete your account for you:

Email us to delete my account

Send it from the Google email you signed in with, or include the phone number you signed in with. We may ask you to confirm the request. We complete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile (name, username, profile photo)
  • Your phone number or email
  • Your settings and alert preferences
  • Your emergency contacts backup
  • Photos you uploaded
  • Your sign-in account

What is kept, and for how long

  • Reports you made stay on the public map as a safety record, but your name, user ID and contact number are removed from them.
  • Some records may be kept for up to 90 days where needed to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud and abuse, or keep the service secure. They are then permanently deleted.
  • Crash and diagnostic data is deleted automatically under Google Firebase's retention settings.

Delete only some data

You can ask us to delete a specific report or a photo you shared, without deleting your account.
